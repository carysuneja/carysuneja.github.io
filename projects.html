Fencing Supply Group

Building common systems and stronger teams

Fencing Supply Group is a distributor of fencing and outdoor living materials with more than 75 branches and manufacturing facilities nationwide.

1. Operating System Implementation

In 2026, I worked with the Service & Operations team to define processes and create standards for branch management of physical inventory, quoting, and open order management. Our team built these standards into an app that allows the branch managers to assess their operations and build an action plan with clear, practical suggestions for improvement, as well as a dashboard that allows for daily performance monitoring. Currently, I am working to create a rollout strategy and agenda that will test a nationwide implementation of these systems and empower the regional service directors to implement operating rhythms and daily branch meetings to review performance. This work includes a focus on developing branch managers’ abilities to create a collaborative branch culture that can create positive change, as well as building the training materials and strategies to teach new processes to the sales representatives and yard associates. The pilot rollout is scheduled for January, 2027 and will be completed across all branches by the end of April.

2. Rapid Improvement Workshop Facilitation

When business conditions dictated a clear need to reduce the quote turnaround time, I designed and facilitated a three-day workout event to bring a cross-regional and cross-functional team together to design an ideal future state. I led the team through a defined process to evaluate the current state, investigate root causes, and plan improvements that were grounded in FSG’s capabilities and future direction. On day three of the event, the team presented the future state to senior leadership, who in turn dedicated the necessary resources to accomplish the plan. In the three months after the workshop, teams completed all of the action items and launched a suite of tools and processes to make the work of the inside sales representatives easier and more efficient.

3. New Hire Product and ERP Training

One of the biggest needs that came out of the rapid improvement workshop was for training on FSG’s products and nomenclature for those products in the ERP system. This need was especially acute for newly hired salespeople and new hires in administrative and support roles outside of the branches. I assembled a team of subject matter experts, designed a curriculum framework, and gathered content from vendors, supply chain, and our internal manufacturing facilities. The team of experts reviewed the content for each training module before I created the videos, narration, and quizzes. The training, comprised of 15 videos and 8 quizzes, is now in FSG’s LMS and is auto-assigned as part of the standard onboarding package for all incoming employees. It not only orients new hires to the range of fencing and decking products FSG sells, it creates a standard language and common understanding of product descriptions in the ERP as well—an especially important consideration for a business with multiple operating companies and product specialties.

4. Leader Onboarding

At Fencing Supply Group, I directed a year-long project to define the processes branch managers use to run the company’s 70+ branches across the 14 operating companies under the FSG umbrella. I spent more than 10 months travelling to FSG branches around the country to create the procedures and documentation to standardize terminology and capture best practices. I shepherded that documentation through an extensive internal review process, then worked with a cross-functional team to turn the procedures into a comprehensive leader onboarding process, including a mentor and buddy program and sequenced skills checklist to structure the first 14 weeks of a new branch manager’s employment. The onboarding process is customized to each region and managed by the regional service directors, but the new hires are responsible for ensuring that they receive orientation and training for each skill on the checklist. To date, more than 15 new branch managers have onboarded with the new system; interview data shows a faster time-to-competence and higher job satisfaction.

5. Leadership Development

FSG undertook a major effort to provide professional development for its branch managers and regional leadership in 2022. I led that effort and started with an interview-based needs assessment with the branch managers to learn about their lived experience and uncover their training and skill development priorities. I developed a three-day, in-person training course that balanced people management with process management and included discussions of Radical Candor and employee retention, as well as discussions of data analysis and process design. I used a scenario-based instructional design, in which small groups discussed a real-life situation with no one right answer and debated what action they would take. Those discussions yielded rich insights and a more powerful learning experience than a PowerPoint presentation could achieve. After each of the five 30-person cohorts attended training, the managers came back together for monthly discussions to share their experiences implementing the new skills, offer advice to their peers, and refresh the content with experts from the executive team.

FlowOne Lean Consulting

Consulting and authorship in healthcare

FlowOne is a software and consulting firm based in Wisconsin that provides patient tracking software and process consulting to ambulatory care clinics in healthcare.

Consultant and Author: Lean Doctors, Make Your Clinics Flow with Synchrony

I co-authored two books on the application of Lean Manufacturing principles to ambulatory clinics in healthcare. Lean Doctors lays out the steps pioneered by FlowOne to transform any clinic process (and really, any process) using Orthopedics at Children’s Wisconsin as a case study. Lean Doctors has been translated into two languages; a new edition and a Spanish version will be published in 2027. I co-authored Make Your Clinics Flow with Synchrony with Dr. Dennis Han; it provides an in-depth look at level loading process steps across job titles and uses ophthalmology as its primary environment. In addition to these publications, I also wrote a guide to Lean implementation for the American Academy of Ophthalmology.

Community involvement

Cream City Gardens

Volunteer Curriculum Developer, Cream City Gardens at The Guest House of Milwaukee

In 2012, I saw staff from the Guest House, a homeless shelter and social services hub for men experiencing homelessness, building raised garden beds on an empty lot across the street from the shelter. I pulled over to ask what they were doing and found out they were starting a gardening program to provide fresh food for the neighborhood and to potentially provide employment for residents. I immediately volunteered. Together, we built a job-training curriculum with master gardeners and employers in the area that taught technical and employment skills and required regular work shifts in the gardens. I know this example is an older one, but watching our first participants graduate from the program and knowing what the accomplishment and recognition meant to them remains one of my professional highlights.
