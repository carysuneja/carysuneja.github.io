Selected work

Featured projects.

Examples of how I combine training, facilitation, leadership development, and process design to make work easier and more effective.

Fencing Supply Group

Building common systems and stronger teams

Fencing Supply Group distributes fencing and outdoor living materials through more than 75 branches and manufacturing facilities nationwide.

Operations · 2026–2027

Operating System Implementation

In 2026, I worked with the Service & Operations team to define standards for physical inventory, quoting, and open order management. We incorporated those standards into an app for branch self-assessment and action planning, along with a dashboard for daily performance monitoring.

I am also supporting the rollout strategy, regional leadership routines, daily performance discussions, and training for sales representatives and yard associates. The pilot is scheduled for January 2027, with rollout across all branches planned by the end of April 2027.

Facilitation · Continuous improvement

Rapid Improvement Workshop Facilitation

To address quote turnaround time, I designed and facilitated a three-day, cross-regional and cross-functional workshop. The group examined current workflows, investigated root causes, and designed an achievable future state.

After the team presented its recommendations to senior leadership, resources were committed. Within three months, the teams completed the workshop's action items and introduced tools and processes that made inside sales work more efficient.

Instructional design

New Hire Product and ERP Training

After the improvement workshop identified a need for product and ERP terminology training, I assembled subject matter experts, designed a curriculum framework, and drew on resources from vendors, supply chain, and manufacturing.

I developed 15 videos and 8 quizzes for the company's learning management system. The training is automatically assigned to incoming employees and establishes a shared vocabulary across operating companies and product specialties.

Manager development

Leader Onboarding

I led a year-long effort to document and standardize the work of branch managers across FSG's operating companies. After extensive branch visits and internal review, a cross-functional team translated those procedures into a structured onboarding experience.

The program includes mentors, buddies, and a sequenced skills checklist for a new manager's first 14 weeks. More than 15 managers have completed onboarding through the system; interview feedback indicates faster time to competence and higher job satisfaction.

Leadership learning · 2022

Leadership Development

I began with interviews of branch managers about their experiences and development priorities, then designed a three-day, in-person course balancing people management and process management.

Scenario-based discussions engaged five cohorts of approximately 30 managers each. Monthly follow-up conversations gave participants a space to apply skills, learn from peers, and engage with executive leadership.

FlowOne Lean Consulting

Consulting and authorship in healthcare

Lean Doctors

I co-authored Lean Doctors, which explains FlowOne's approach to improving clinic processes, using an orthopedic clinic at Children's Wisconsin as a case study. The book has been translated into two languages; a new edition and Spanish version are planned for 2027.

Make Your Clinics Flow with Synchrony

Co-authored with Dr. Dennis Han, this book examines balancing process steps across job titles, with ophthalmology as its primary setting. I also wrote a guide to Lean implementation for the American Academy of Ophthalmology.

Community involvement

Cream City Gardens

Volunteer Curriculum Developer · The Guest House of Milwaukee

In 2012, I volunteered with a gardening initiative connected to the Guest House, a Milwaukee shelter and social-services organization. Working with master gardeners and area employers, we developed job training that combined technical and employment skills with regular garden shifts.

Seeing the first participants graduate, and recognizing what that achievement meant to them, remains one of my professional highlights.
