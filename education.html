Education

Curiosity put to work.

University of Wisconsin- Milwaukee, Master of Liberal Studies, Doctor of Philosophy

I returned to graduate school after a consulting career with FlowOne, first earning a second master’s degree as part of the Liberal Studies program at UWM, and completing a PhD in 2022. While working on my PhD, I taught at Carroll University and renewed my love of working with students in a classroom setting. As a dissertator, I was awarded a fellowship for writing excellence—a gratifying outcome after two years of pandemic-era writing in isolation. I was offered the opportunity to design leadership training at Fencing Supply Group shortly before graduation in 2022, and was able to combine teaching, training, writing, and group facilitation at FSG.

Marquette University, Master of Arts

At Marquette, I was formally trained to teach as part of a full-tuition graduate assistantship I was awarded for graduate study. This training involved two weeks of pedagogy coursework plus two semesters of ongoing practicum discussions on the process of creating a curriculum, working with students, and managing a classroom. After graduation, I stayed on a Marquette as a faculty member in the Educational Opportunity Program, working with first generation students and English Language Learners to ensure their success studying at the college level. The training I received at Marquette was reinforced and expanded in my early career teaching at Milwaukee Area Technical College as part of the Workforce Development Institute, teaching basic skills and computer use at learning centers at PPG Industries (where I went on to a 12-year career in Organizational Development and quality), Harley Davidson, and Delphi Electronics.
