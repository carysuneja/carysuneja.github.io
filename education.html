Education and teaching

Curiosity put to work.

My academic experience informs the way I design learning, communicate ideas, and help groups make progress together.

University of Wisconsin–Milwaukee

Doctor of Philosophy (2022) & Master of Liberal Studies

I returned to graduate school following my career at FlowOne, earning a second master's degree through UWM's Liberal Studies program before completing my PhD in 2022.

While working on the doctorate, I taught at Carroll University and rediscovered my love of working with students. As a dissertator, I received a fellowship for writing excellence. Shortly before graduation, I began the opportunity to develop leadership training at Fencing Supply Group, bringing together teaching, writing, training, and facilitation.

Marquette University

Master of Arts

At Marquette, I received a full-tuition graduate assistantship that included formal preparation for teaching: intensive pedagogy coursework and two semesters of ongoing practicum discussions about curriculum, students, and classroom management.

After graduation, I worked as a faculty member in Marquette's Educational Opportunity Program, supporting first-generation college students and English Language Learners.

Teaching and workforce development

Learning in many settings

I further developed my teaching practice at Milwaukee Area Technical College's Workforce Development Institute, where I taught foundational skills and computer use in learning centers at PPG Industries, Harley-Davidson, and Delphi Electronics. At PPG, that work led to a 12-year career in organizational development and quality.
