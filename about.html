About Cary

Learning, leading, improving.

My career has centered on the relationship between how work is organized and how people experience it.

I'm a lifelong trainer, teacher, and improver of processes, teams, and organizations. The work I love best is solving problems that get in the way of fulfilling work: inefficient workflows, unclear communication or authority, and gaps in the skills needed to lead people and work.

I work best when I understand what an organization values and can draw on its formal and informal knowledge to bring better ways of working to the surface.

My professional journey

PPG Industries · Manufacturing and training

I began my career at PPG Industries, working in Oak Creek, Wisconsin; Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania; and Fort Myers, Florida. The company's established processes and expertise gave me opportunities to connect work design with employees' experiences and to learn how to analyze work at the task level. During my time as training manager in Oak Creek, the plant won the Wisconsin Manufacturer of the Year Award twice for training initiatives.

As Global Quality Training Manager, I earned Lean Manufacturing certification from the University of Michigan and worked internationally with leaders and teams. I launched online learning on policy deployment for PPG's top 6,000 managers, earning a Training Magazine E-Learning Award for instructional design.

Later, at PPG's automotive replacement glass call center in Fort Myers, I helped modernize onboarding and just-in-time training, transitioning parts of the program online and building instructional design capabilities within the training team.

FlowOne · Healthcare process consulting

Over seven years with FlowOne, I worked with healthcare clients and strengthened my practice in facilitation and process improvement. The experience reinforced how many critical elements of successful work processes carry across industries.

Fencing Supply Group · Service & Operations

At Fencing Supply Group, I apply these skills with a small Service & Operations team that serves as an internal consulting resource. Our work includes evaluating job structures, equipping teams with better tools, and aligning acquired businesses around common operating practices and goals.

The through line

I believe the most durable improvements come from listening closely, understanding the work, and helping the people who do it lead change themselves.
